# Testing Focus Mode

1. Complete a symptom check-in, then enable **Focus Mode** from the top bar.
2. Sit normally during the same-session calibration/reference period.
3. After calibration, a single blink, quick look-away, or brief posture change is ignored.
4. To test deliberately, sustain **close viewing + squinting together for roughly 3–5 seconds** after calibration. This should qualify as two independent signal families and trigger a matched reading-support decision.
5. With **Automatically apply matching reading support** off, SomatoSync asks before changing the interface. With it on, the matched change applies immediately and a visible **Undo** state appears instead of a second approval prompt.
6. Applied changes are symptom-specific and reversible: close viewing + squinting can enter Reading Spotlight; meaningful light sensitivity can change the visual palette/media intensity; mental fatigue can reduce secondary detail; motion discomfort can reduce movement.
7. If the SomatoSync tab becomes inactive, camera inference and session timing pause. When you return, SomatoSync gives a short ~3-second settling period before evaluating reading behavior again. Tab switching itself is not treated as a symptom.
8. Mark prompts as accurate or false alarms when offered so local personalization can improve over repeated confirmed sessions.

Focus Mode is an experimental accessibility aid, not a diagnostic test. Raw video and face landmarks are not stored.
