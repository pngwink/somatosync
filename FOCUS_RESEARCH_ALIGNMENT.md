# Focus Mode Research Alignment

This version refines Focus around lower-risk, symptom-matched accommodations and avoids prescribing a universal color treatment.

## Evidence-informed mapping

- Light sensitivity: lower page luminance, remove bright backgrounds/patterns, calm media, preserve readable contrast; do not prescribe a universal hue.
- Visual/reading difficulty: moderately enlarge reading text, increase line spacing, constrain line length, and strengthen hierarchy.
- Dizziness / visual-motion discomfort: reduce animation, autoplay and unnecessary viewport motion.
- Cognitive fatigue: simplify hierarchy, space sections, focus the reading lane, and surface pacing/break support rather than hiding critical information.
- Rereading / sustained close-viewing + squint: strengthen spacing, typography, and reading focus. The in-app automatic reading ruler is disabled in Version 6; Shield retains a user-controlled reading spotlight.
- Live camera signals remain supportive heuristics, not diagnostic findings.

The UI never uses whole-page browser zoom and preserves navigation. External-site changes remain consent-first per origin.
