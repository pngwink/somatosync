# Version Notes — Final Version (Version 10): Focus Cognitive-Load Refinement

Building on Version 9, this is the final version in the repository sequence. It makes stronger Focus adaptations change the amount and structure of information on screen, not just typography or color.

## Changes in this version

- Added progressive disclosure for long ordinary body paragraphs during strong Focus, with **Show full text / Show less** controls so the original content remains available.
- Excludes safety content, urgent guidance, alerts, dialogs, forms, and explicitly preserved clinical text from automatic collapsing.
- Allows a sustained close-viewing + squint pattern, after the existing multi-signal persistence gates qualify it, to trigger stronger density reduction alongside reading-text and spacing changes.
- Turns strong Focus into a more single-task layout by collapsing the desktop sidebar and nonessential header chrome while keeping navigation available through the header menu.
- Reflows multi-column content into a narrower one-column reading lane and reduces decorative chrome during strong cognitive-load support.
- Replaces the prior teal/green Focus treatment with neutral stone/graphite palettes so no hue is presented as concussion treatment.
- Keeps Reading Spotlight manual and reversible rather than claiming eye tracking or inferring what a person is reading.

## Safety / scope

Focus remains an experimental accessibility aid. MediaPipe-derived interaction patterns are nonspecific; they do not diagnose concussion, measure neurological fatigue or severity, or determine readiness. Stronger adaptations remain reversible and preserve safety-critical information.
